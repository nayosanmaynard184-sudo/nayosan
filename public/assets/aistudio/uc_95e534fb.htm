University of the Cordilleras

University of the Cordilleras

YesUCan

Nestled in the heart of Baguio City—a UNESCO Creative City and the ‘Summer Capital’ of the Philippines—the University of the Cordilleras offers a premier English-medium education characterized by an eternal spring climate and a deeply rooted highland heritage. As a multi-awarded institution with top-tier programs in Information Technology, Teacher Education, and Criminology, UC provides students a safe, walkable campus environment where cutting-edge academic innovation meets the vibrant indigenous culture of the Cordilleras. It is the ideal destination for students seeking high-quality learning and meaningful cultural immersion in one of the country’s most iconic mountain retreats.
